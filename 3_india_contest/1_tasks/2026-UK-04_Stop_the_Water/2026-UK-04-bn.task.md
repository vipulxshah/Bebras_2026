## Body

বাবলু কিছু পাইপ নিয়ে খেলছিল আর সেগুলোর ভেতর জল ঢালছিল। পাইপগুলোর মাঝে A থেকে I পর্যন্ত নাম দেওয়া কিছু কল (ট্যাপ) আছে, যেগুলো খুলে বা বন্ধ করে জলের যাওয়া-আসা নিয়ন্ত্রণ করা যায়। কিন্তু সমস্যা হলো, A নম্বর কলটি নষ্ট হয়ে গেছে এবং ওটা কোনোভাবেই বন্ধ করা যাচ্ছে না!

## Answer Options/Interactivity Description

A) A এবং C নম্বর কল
B) C এবং F নম্বর কল
C) B, C এবং F নম্বর কল
D) B এবং C নম্বর কল



## Answer Explanation

The correct answer is (D).

![ansD]

Two paths lead the water to Tap E: A -> B -> E and C -> E. Since Tap A is broken, then closing B and C blocks the path to E using the least amount of taps.

Answer A is incorrect

![ansA]

Closing A and C would block both paths to E but the question states that tap A is broken.

Answer B is incorrect

![ansB]

Closing C blocks one path, but water still reaches E by traveling A -> B -> E.

Answer C is incorrect

![ansC]

Closing B and C does block both paths to E, but F is unnecessary.


## This is Informatics

In this task, you explored how water flows between connected places. The connected pipes form a network. Computers use networks to represent many systems in real life, like how cars travel on roads, how water gets to your house and how information travels across the internet. By closing taps, you changed the paths the water could take. This is similar to how computers systems control how information travels across networks, for example, to stop viruses from spreading or to guide messages to the right person.

## This is Computational Thinking

Decomposition – this is used when you breakdown the network of pipes into individual paths and consider how each tap's open/close state affects the flow of water. 

Abstraction – the story context is simplified into a model of connected paths and control points. This requires abstraction to focus only on which pipes connect, the direction of the water flow and the positions of the taps while ignoring the playful setting. 

Logical reasoning – this is used to predict various consequences. For example, if a particular tap is closed, which paths to tap E remain open? Or what is the smallest number of closures that adheres to the constraint that tap A is broken and must stay open?
